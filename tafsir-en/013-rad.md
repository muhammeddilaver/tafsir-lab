# 13. Sūrat al-Raʿd

**Forty-three verses.** Disagreement over the place of revelation is transmitted in the classical sources; **no definite ruling is issued in this commentary.** It takes its name from the word **ٱلرَّعْد** (thunder), which occurs in the thirteenth verse.

## The structure of the sūra

| Block | Verses | Subject | What closes the block |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | 1-4 | The Book and the signs of heaven and earth | *Inna fī dhālika la-āyātin li-qawmin yaʿqilūn* (4) |
| **II** | 5-15 | Objection, the scope of knowledge, thunder | *Wa mā duʿāʾu'l-kāfirīna illā fī ḍalāl* (14) |
| **III** | 16-24 | Two similitudes and the attributes of those who possess understanding | *Fa-niʿma ʿuqba'd-dār* (24) |
| **IV** | 25-35 | Those who break the covenant, and the comparison of the two abodes | *Wa ʿuqba'l-kāfirīna'n-nār* (35) |
| **V** | 36-43 | The Book, erasure and confirmation, and the close | *Wa man ʿindahū ʿilmu'l-kitāb* (43) |

**The backbone of the sūra is *that different outcomes issue from the same conditions*.** This is established in the fourth verse by an observation drawn from agriculture (different crops watered with the same water) and repeated in the seventeenth verse by a similitude of a flood (water and scum in one and the same valley). **All the comparisons of the sūra attach to these two verses.**

## 13/1-4 — ٱلْمٓرٰ … ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِى رَفَعَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ بِغَيْرِ عَمَدٍ تَرَوْنَهَا … وَفِى ٱلْأَرْضِ قِطَعٌ مُّتَجَٰوِرَٰتٌ وَجَنَّٰتٌ مِّنْ أَعْنَٰبٍ وَزَرْعٌ وَنَخِيلٌ صِنْوَانٌ وَغَيْرُ صِنْوَانٍ يُسْقَىٰ بِمَآءٍ وَٰحِدٍ وَنُفَضِّلُ بَعْضَهَا عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍ فِى ٱلْأُكُلِ

"Alif-lām-mīm-rāʾ… **It is God who raised up the heavens without pillars that you can see**… **In the earth there are neighbouring tracts, gardens of vines, fields of grain, and date palms growing several from one root and singly: all are watered with a single water, and yet We make some of them surpass others in what they yield.**"

**The note on the disconnected letters has been entered many times in the index; I do not repeat it.**

**بِغَيْرِ عَمَدٍ تَرَوْنَهَا** — **the construction was treated at `031-lokman.md` 31/10**, and two readings were tabulated there (there are no pillars that you could see / it has pillars but you do not see them). **I do not repeat it, and I hold to the same limit: no conclusion about modern cosmology is to be drawn from this.**

**كُلٌّ يَجْرِى لِأَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى** — **the construction *ajalin musammā* was treated at `046-ahkaf.md` 46/3, `039-zumer.md` 39/42, `035-fatir.md` 35/45, `031-lokman.md` 31/29 and `029-ankebut.md` 29/53.** **In all six places the same thing is said.**

### صِنْوَان — and the key observation of the sūra

**The root ص-ن-و — it occurs here for the first time in the index.** The sense the lexicographers give: ***ṣinw*, more than one stem issuing from a single root;** it is used of the paired stems that part from one root in the date palm. **It is recorded that the word is used for brotherhood as well: *ṣinwu abīk* — your father's brother.**

**And the observation the verse sets up must be recorded:**

| What is shared | What differs |
|---|---|
| *Yusqā bi-māʾin **wāḥid*** — **a single water** | *Nufaḍḍilu baʿḍahā ʿalā baʿḍin fi'l-ukul* — **what they yield** |

**I record this as my own reading, and its ground is the verse's own sentence:** the same soil, the same water, even the same root — **and still a different yield.** **The difference, that is, does not lie in the means that come from outside.**

**And this observation is the frame for the rest of the sūra:** in the sixteenth verse the blind and the seeing, in the seventeenth water and scum, in the nineteenth the one who knows and the blind will be set against each other. **All carry the same structure: the same thing was given, the outcome diverged.**

**At `035-fatir.md` 35/27-28 the variety of colours (*mukhtalifun alwānuhā*) was treated across three domains**, and it was tied there to *ʿulamāʾ*. **The difference in al-Raʿd: there variety is given as a sign, here as a *divergence*.**

## 13/5-7 — وَإِن تَعْجَبْ فَعَجَبٌ قَوْلُهُمْ أَءِذَا كُنَّا تُرَٰبًا أَءِنَّا لَفِى خَلْقٍ جَدِيدٍ … وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ لَوْلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيْهِ ءَايَةٌ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦٓ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُنذِرٌ وَلِكُلِّ قَوْمٍ هَادٍ

"**If you would wonder, then what is truly to be wondered at is this word of theirs: 'When we have become dust, shall we really be in a new creation?'**… Those who disbelieve say: '**Should a miracle not have been sent down upon him from his Lord?**' **You are only a warner; and for every people there is a guide.**"

**وَإِن تَعْجَبْ فَعَجَبٌ قَوْلُهُمْ** — **moving the place of the wonder.**

**The word *ʿajab* was treated at `050-kaf.md` 50/2 (*bal ʿajibū an jāʾahum mundhirun minhum*)**, and it was recorded there: **that the objection begins not with an argument but with astonishment, that is, that *familiarity* is made the measure.** **I rely on that treatment.**

**The difference here must be recorded:** in Qāf it was the other side that was astonished; **in al-Raʿd the astonishment is handed back to the one addressed** — *fa-ʿajabun qawluhum.*

**وَيَسْتَعْجِلُونَكَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ قَبْلَ ٱلْحَسَنَةِ وَقَدْ خَلَتْ مِن قَبْلِهِمُ ٱلْمَثُلَٰتُ** (6) — *al-mathulāt*: **punishments that stand as a lesson.**

**I record this as an observation:** the demand that the punishment be hastened was treated in several places in the index (`034-sebe.md` 34/29, `029-ankebut.md` 29/53). **What al-Raʿd adds: that this is a thing whose precedent is to be found in the past.**

**إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُنذِرٌ وَلِكُلِّ قَوْمٍ هَادٍ** — "**you are only a warner; and for every people there is a guide.**"

**There is disagreement over the second half of the sentence:**

| Reading | Who the *hād* is |
|---|---|
| 1 | **A warner sent to every people** — the same line as Fāṭir 35/24 |
| 2 | **The guide is God** |

**Both readings are transmitted; I do not impose a preference.** **The principle that a warner has been sent was treated at `035-fatir.md` 35/24 and `028-kasas.md` 28/59;** the first reading sits on that line.

## 13/8-11 — ٱللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مَا تَحْمِلُ كُلُّ أُنثَىٰ وَمَا تَغِيضُ ٱلْأَرْحَامُ وَمَا تَزْدَادُ وَكُلُّ شَىْءٍ عِندَهُۥ بِمِقْدَارٍ … إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوْمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُوا۟ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمْ

"**God knows what every female bears, and what the wombs diminish and what they increase; with Him everything is by a measure**… **God does not change what is in a people until they change what is in themselves.**"

**مَا تَغِيضُ ٱلْأَرْحَامُ وَمَا تَزْدَادُ** — *ghayḍ*: **to diminish, to recede** (it is used of water receding). **Per the method (`STYLE.md`): I draw no conclusion about modern embryology from this;** the verse states **the scope of the knowledge**. **The same field was treated at `041-fussilet.md` 41/47 and `035-fatir.md` 35/11;** I rely on that treatment.

**بِمِقْدَارٍ** — **the root ق-د-ر was treated at `097-kadr.md` and `054-kamer.md` 54/49** — the core sense is **measure**.

### مُعَقِّبَٰتٌ مِّنۢ بَيْنِ يَدَيْهِ وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِۦ يَحْفَظُونَهُۥ مِنْ أَمْرِ ٱللَّهِ (11)

The root **ع-ق-ب**: **to come after, to follow in another's track.** *Muʿaqqibāt* — **those who come one after another.**

**The construction *min bayni yadayhi wa min khalfihi* was treated in many places in the index** (`041-fussilet.md` 41/25 and 41/42, `019-meryem.md` 19/64): **to encompass every side of a thing.**

### إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوْمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُوا۟ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمْ

**The structure of the sentence must be recorded, and this is an observation about the language:**

| Side | Verb | Object |
|---|---|---|
| God | *Lā yughayyiru* | *Mā bi-qawm* — **the condition a people is in** |
| The people | *Yughayyirū* | *Mā bi-anfusihim* — **what is in themselves** |

**The two objects are different: one is *bi-qawm* (the state of the people), the other *bi-anfusihim* (what is in themselves).**

**I record this as my own reading, and its ground is that the two constructions differ:** the verse ties the change of the outward condition to **the change of the inward one**. **And the order runs one way only: what is inside comes first.**

**A note, per the method (`STYLE.md`):** this verse is not to be pressed into current political argument, and it is not so used in this commentary. **The context of the verse must also be recorded:** the sentence occurs inside a verse in which the guardians are named, and it runs on with *wa idhā arāda'llāhu bi-qawmin sūʾan fa-lā maradda lah*. **That is: in stating the responsibility of the human being, the sentence does not say that the outcome lies absolutely with the human being.**

## 13/12-13 — هُوَ ٱلَّذِى يُرِيكُمُ ٱلْبَرْقَ خَوْفًا وَطَمَعًا وَيُنشِئُ ٱلسَّحَابَ ٱلثِّقَالَ · وَيُسَبِّحُ ٱلرَّعْدُ بِحَمْدِهِۦ وَٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ مِنْ خِيفَتِهِۦ

"**It is He who shows you the lightning, as fear and as hope, and who raises up the heavy clouds. The thunder glorifies Him with praise, and the angels too, out of fear of Him.**"

**خَوْفًا وَطَمَعًا** — **two opposed feelings, in a single event.** **The same expression was treated at `030-rum.md` 30/24** (*yurīkumu'l-barqa khawfan wa ṭamaʿā*). **I rely on that treatment.**

**I record this as an observation:** for one who lives in the desert, lightning is both the danger of the thunderbolt and the good news of rain. **The verse does not separate the two — it gives two responses to one and the same event.**

### وَيُسَبِّحُ ٱلرَّعْدُ بِحَمْدِهِ

**This is the verse that gives the sūra its name.**

**The root ر-ع-د — it occurs here for the first time in the index:** **to rumble, to tremble.** The lexicographers record the sense of "shaking, trembling" in the root (*irʿāda* — trembling).

**And the verb must be recorded: *yusabbiḥu* — it glorifies.**

**A limit, per the method (`STYLE.md`):** I draw no claim about meteorology from this. **This question was treated at `017-isra.md` 17/44 (*wa in min shayʾin illā yusabbiḥu bi-ḥamdihi wa lākin lā tafqahūna tasbīḥahum*);** **the verse itself says that this glorification is not understood.**

**وَهُمْ يُجَٰدِلُونَ فِى ٱللَّهِ وَهُوَ شَدِيدُ ٱلْمِحَالِ** — **the root ج-د-ل was the backbone of `040-gafir.md`** (five occurrences were tabulated there) **and at `018-kehf.md` 18/54 it was named as the most marked trait of the human being.** **I rely on that treatment.**

**And the word order is worth recording:** the glorification of the thunder and the disputing of the human being stand side by side **in the same verse**.

## 13/14 — لَهُۥ دَعْوَةُ ٱلْحَقِّ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ لَا يَسْتَجِيبُونَ لَهُم بِشَىْءٍ إِلَّا كَبَٰسِطِ كَفَّيْهِ إِلَى ٱلْمَآءِ لِيَبْلُغَ فَاهُ وَمَا هُوَ بِبَٰلِغِهِۦ

"**The true call is to Him. Those they call upon besides Him answer them with nothing; they are like a man stretching out his palms towards water so that it may reach his mouth — and it will not reach it.**"

### The similitude taken apart

**The comparison is worth recording, and its elements can be separated one by one:**

| Element | What it is |
|---|---|
| *Bāsiṭu kaffayh* | **The one who spreads out his palms** — there is movement |
| *Ila'l-māʾ* | **Towards the water** — the target is real |
| *Li-yablugha fāh* | **So that it may reach his mouth** — the aim is legitimate |
| *Wa mā huwa bi-bālighih* | **It will not reach** — the one thing that is missing |

**I record this as my own reading:** what is wrong in the similitude is **neither the intention nor the target nor the effort** — the water really is there, and the man really does reach out. **What is missing is that the water does not come to the mouth of itself.** The call, that is, is made not into a void but **to a place that cannot answer**.

**And the same matter was treated in three stages at `035-fatir.md` 35/13-14** (they do not hear; if they heard they could not answer; in the end they reject). **Al-Raʿd gives the same thing in a single similitude.**

**وَمَا دُعَآءُ ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَ إِلَّا فِى ضَلَٰلٍ** — *ḍalāl*: **being lost, straying from the way.** **The prayer, that is, is not lost — it loses *its way*.**

## 13/15-16 — وَلِلَّهِ يَسْجُدُ مَن فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ طَوْعًا وَكَرْهًا وَظِلَٰلُهُم بِٱلْغُدُوِّ وَٱلْـَٔاصَالِ · … قُلْ هَلْ يَسْتَوِى ٱلْأَعْمَىٰ وَٱلْبَصِيرُ أَمْ هَلْ تَسْتَوِى ٱلظُّلُمَٰتُ وَٱلنُّورُ

"**Whoever is in the heavens and the earth prostrates to God — willingly or unwillingly — and their shadows too, morning and evening.** Say: '**Are the blind and the seeing equal? Or are the darknesses and the light equal?**'"

### طَوْعًا وَكَرْهًا

**This pair was treated at `041-fussilet.md` 41/11** (*iʾtiyā ṭawʿan aw karhan*), and it was recorded there: **that a command offering two options is answered in one direction only** (*ataynā ṭāʾiʿīn*). **I rely on that treatment.**

**The difference here must be recorded:**

| Place | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Fuṣṣilat 41/11 | *Ṭawʿan **aw** karhan* — **an option** (either … or) |
| **Al-Raʿd 13/15** | *Ṭawʿan **wa** karhan* — **both alike** |

**In Fuṣṣilat the offer had two alternatives; in al-Raʿd the statement covers both sides.** I record this as an observation about the language.

**وَظِلَٰلُهُم بِٱلْغُدُوِّ وَٱلْـَٔاصَالِ** — **and the shadows too are named.**

**The sign of the shadow was treated in detail at `025-furkan.md` 25/45-46** (*kayfa madda'ẓ-ẓill*) and taken up again at `016-nahl.md` 16/48 (*yatafayyaʾu ẓilāluhu*). **I rely on that treatment.**

**I record this as my own reading:** among the things that prostrate, **shadows** are counted — that is, a thing that has no existence of its own, that exists only through the existence of something else. **And the movement of a shadow is not in the hands of the person.** This is the point of its standing in the list of those that prostrate.

### هَلْ يَسْتَوِى ٱلْأَعْمَىٰ وَٱلْبَصِيرُ

**The "they are not equal" series was treated in many places in the index:** `040-gafir.md` 40/58, `045-casiye.md` 45/21, `035-fatir.md` 35/19-22, `032-secde.md` 32/18. **Al-Raʿd is one link in that chain.**

**And its place within the sūra must be recorded:** this comparison comes **immediately before the flood similitude of the seventeenth verse**. **The question is put, that is, and then answered with a similitude.**

## 13/17 — أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءً فَسَالَتْ أَوْدِيَةٌۢ بِقَدَرِهَا فَٱحْتَمَلَ ٱلسَّيْلُ زَبَدًا رَّابِيًا … كَذَٰلِكَ يَضْرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلْحَقَّ وَٱلْبَٰطِلَ فَأَمَّا ٱلزَّبَدُ فَيَذْهَبُ جُفَآءً وَأَمَّا مَا يَنفَعُ ٱلنَّاسَ فَيَمْكُثُ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ

"**He sent down water from the sky, and the valleys flowed each according to its own measure; and the torrent carried a swelling scum**… **Thus does God set forth the true and the false: the scum is cast off and goes; what benefits people stays in the earth.**"

### Two similitudes, one verse

**The verse sets up two separate comparisons: the scum of the torrent and the dross of metal** (*wa mimmā yūqidūna ʿalayhi fi'n-nāri'btighāʾa ḥilyatin aw matāʿin zabadun mithluh*).

**And what the two share must be recorded: the scum stands *on top* and is what is *visible*.**

| Element | The scum | What remains |
|---|---|---|
| Position | **On top** — *rābiyan* | Below / in the earth |
| Visibility | **What is seen** | What is not seen |
| Duration | *Yadhhabu jufāʾan* — **it is cast off and goes** | *Yamkuthu fi'l-arḍ* — **it stays** |
| Measure | — | *Mā yanfaʿu'n-nās* — **benefit** |

**جُفَآء** — root **ج-ف-أ**: **to throw, to cast out.** The word occurs only here in the Qurʾān.

**I record this as my own reading, and its ground is the elements of the similitude:** the verse separates the true from the false not by **momentary visibility** but by **what lasts**. **And the measure set down is *mā yanfaʿu'n-nās* (what benefits people)** — the distinction, that is, lies not in the claim itself but in what it leaves behind.

**فَسَالَتْ أَوْدِيَةٌۢ بِقَدَرِهَا** — "**the valleys flowed each according to its own measure.**"

**This detail is worth recording:** the water is one, **the valleys different.** **And this is the same observation as that of the fourth verse: a single water, a different yield.** It is a repetition that can be verified within the sūra.

**The similitude of water, plant and withering was treated at `018-kehf.md` 18/45 and `039-zumer.md` 39/21;** **what sets al-Raʿd apart is that its subject is not the water itself but *the scum the water carries*.**

## 13/18 — لِلَّذِينَ ٱسْتَجَابُوا۟ لِرَبِّهِمُ ٱلْحُسْنَىٰ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَمْ يَسْتَجِيبُوا۟ لَهُۥ لَوْ أَنَّ لَهُم مَّا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ جَمِيعًا وَمِثْلَهُۥ مَعَهُۥ لَٱفْتَدَوْا۟ بِهِۦٓ

"**For those who answer their Lord there is the best. As for those who do not answer Him: if all that is in the earth were theirs, and the like of it with it, they would offer the whole of it as a ransom.**"

The measure must be recorded: *mā fi'l-arḍi jamīʿan wa mithlahu maʿah* — the whole of the earth **and the like of it again.**

**The same measure was treated at `039-zumer.md` 39/47**, and it was recorded there: **an amount that goes beyond what exists — and even so it is not accepted.** **I rely on that treatment.**

**And the difference between the two verses is worth recording:** in al-Zumar the ransom was offered **to escape the punishment**; **in al-Raʿd the context of the ransom is not having answered the call.**

**I record this as my own reading, and its ground is the first half of the verse:** what was given to the other side was *al-ḥusnā* (the best), and **the only thing asked in return was *istijāba* (answering the call)** — not a price, that is, but a response. **The scene of the ransom shows what follows from that response not having been given.**

## 13/19-24 — أَفَمَن يَعْلَمُ أَنَّمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيْكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ ٱلْحَقُّ كَمَنْ هُوَ أَعْمَىٰٓ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُو۟لُوا۟ ٱلْأَلْبَٰبِ

"**Is the one who knows that what has been sent down to you from your Lord is the truth like the one who is blind? Only those who possess understanding take heed.**"

### The list of attributes

**The verses that follow enumerate the attributes of *ulu'l-albāb*, and the list can be set out as a table:**

| Verse | Attribute |
|---|---|
| 20 | *Yūfūna bi-ʿahdi'llāhi wa lā yanquḍūna'l-mīthāq* — **keeping the covenant** |
| 21 | *Yaṣilūna mā amara'llāhu bihi an yūṣal* — **joining what has been commanded to be joined** |
| 22 | *Ṣabarū'btighāʾa wajhi rabbihim* — **patience** |
| 22 | *Wa anfaqū mimmā razaqnāhum sirran wa ʿalāniya* — **spending in secret and in the open** |
| 22 | *Wa yadraʾūna bi'l-ḥasanati's-sayyiʾa* — **repelling the evil with the good** |

***Sirran wa ʿalāniya* was treated at `035-fatir.md` 35/29 and at `002-bakara.md` 2/271.**

***Wa yadraʾūna bi'l-ḥasanati's-sayyiʾa* — it occurs word for word at `028-kasas.md` 28/54**, and a table covering three sūras was set up there (`041-fussilet.md` 41/34, `029-ankebut.md` 29/46, `028-kasas.md` 28/54). **This is the fourth link.**

**يَصِلُونَ مَآ أَمَرَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦٓ أَن يُوصَلَ** — "**they join what God has commanded to be joined.**"

**The object of the sentence is not specified.** It is widely transmitted that the classical commentaries explain it as **the bond of kinship**. **I transmit this;** and I record the following as an observation: **by leaving the object open, the verse widens the scope.**

**أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمْ عُقْبَى ٱلدَّارِ** — *ʿuqba'd-dār*: **the end of the abode, its outcome.** **The construction occurs twice in the sūra (22 and 24) and returns in the thirty-fifth verse with its opposite: *wa ʿuqba'l-kāfirīna'n-nār*.**

## 13/25-27 — وَٱلَّذِينَ يَنقُضُونَ عَهْدَ ٱللَّهِ مِنۢ بَعْدِ مِيثَٰقِهِۦ وَيَقْطَعُونَ مَآ أَمَرَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦٓ أَن يُوصَلَ وَيُفْسِدُونَ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ … ٱللَّهُ يَبْسُطُ ٱلرِّزْقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقْدِرُ

"**Those who break God's covenant after its confirmation, who cut what God has commanded to be joined, and who work corruption in the earth…**"

### The reverse of the twentieth verse

**This verse is built as the exact opposite of the twentieth and twenty-first verses, and it can be compared word for word:**

| Verses 20-21 | **Verse 25** |
|---|---|
| *Yūfūna bi-ʿahdi'llāh* — **keeping the covenant** | *Yanquḍūna ʿahda'llāh* — **breaking the covenant** |
| *Wa lā yanquḍūna'l-mīthāq* | *Min baʿdi mīthāqih* |
| *Yaṣilūna mā amara'llāhu bihi an yūṣal* | *Wa yaqṭaʿūna mā amara'llāhu bihi an yūṣal* |

**In all three items the same words, with opposite verbs.** This is a symmetry that can be verified within the sūra, and I record this as my own reading: **the sūra does not describe two groups separately — it reads one and the same list in two directions.**

**ٱللَّهُ يَبْسُطُ ٱلرِّزْقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقْدِرُ** (26) — **two verbs: to spread out and to give by measure.**

**The same pair was treated at `039-zumer.md` 39/52 and `042-sura.md` 42/27**, and it was recorded there: **abundance is no proof of merit, nor straitness a proof of having fallen from favour.** **I rely on that treatment.**

**وَفَرِحُوا۟ بِٱلْحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنْيَا وَمَا ٱلْحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنْيَا فِى ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ إِلَّا مَتَٰعٌ** — **the root ف-ر-ح was treated at `040-gafir.md` 40/75 (*bimā kuntum tafraḥūna … wa bimā kuntum tamraḥūn*)**, and the difference between *faraḥ* and *maraḥ* was given there. **I rely on that treatment.**

**قُلْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُضِلُّ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهْدِىٓ إِلَيْهِ مَنْ أَنَابَ** (27) — **the second half of the sentence carries a condition: *man anāb* — the one who turns.**

**The root أ-ن-ب was treated at `050-kaf.md` 50/8 and `040-gafir.md` 40/13.** **And what was recorded there holds here too: the sign is shown to everyone, while the taking of the lesson is tied to turning.**

## 13/28 — ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَتَطْمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكْرِ ٱللَّهِ أَلَا بِذِكْرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ ٱلْقُلُوبُ

"**Those who believe — their hearts come to rest in the remembrance of God. Know that it is in the remembrance of God that hearts come to rest.**"

**The root ط-م-ن was analysed at `002-bakara.md` 2/260** (to settle, to come to rest in its place; the picture the lexicographers record of sitting down into a hollow and being fixed there) **and the difference between belief and *iṭmiʾnān* was recorded there: the one is acceptance, the other a settling inward.** **I rely on that treatment.**

**And the sentence being said twice is worth recording:**

| Sentence | Pattern |
|---|---|
| *Alladhīna āmanū wa taṭmaʾinnu qulūbuhum bi-dhikri'llāh* | **Description** — who they are |
| *Alā bi-dhikri'llāhi taṭmaʾinnu'l-qulūb* | **Ruling** — reinforced with *alā* |

**In the second sentence there is fronting: *bi-dhikri'llāhi* has been brought forward.** **In Arabic this conveys restriction: not with anything else.**

## 13/29-30 — ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ طُوبَىٰ لَهُمْ وَحُسْنُ مَـَٔابٍ · كَذَٰلِكَ أَرْسَلْنَٰكَ فِىٓ أُمَّةٍ قَدْ خَلَتْ مِن قَبْلِهَآ أُمَمٌ لِّتَتْلُوَا۟ عَلَيْهِمُ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَوْحَيْنَآ إِلَيْكَ وَهُمْ يَكْفُرُونَ بِٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ

"**Blessed are those who believe and do righteous deeds; theirs is a good place of return.** Thus have We sent you to a community before which many communities have passed away… **while they deny al-Raḥmān.**"

**طُوبَىٰ** — root **ط-ي-ب**: to be pleasant, clean, good. **The word occurs only here in the Qurʾān**, and the lexicographers explain it as **"goodness, pleasantness; a good state"**. **In some explanations it is transmitted as the name of a tree in the Garden; I transmit this report, and I do not present it as established knowledge.**

**وَهُمْ يَكْفُرُونَ بِٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ** — "**they deny al-Raḥmān.**"

**That the name *al-Raḥmān* became a matter of dispute was treated in detail at `025-furkan.md` 25/60 (*wa ma'r-Raḥmān*) and in `019-meryem.md` (the sixteen occurrences of the name).** **I rely on that treatment.**

**And the word order is worth recording:** after stating the messenger's task, the sentence adds the condition of the other side **with a circumstantial clause**. **The reciting goes on, that is, and the denial goes on too** — the two at the same time.

**قُلْ هُوَ رَبِّى لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَإِلَيْهِ مَتَابِ** — and the answer is given without entering the dispute, by a statement of position.

---

## 13/31 — وَلَوْ أَنَّ قُرْءَانًا سُيِّرَتْ بِهِ ٱلْجِبَالُ أَوْ قُطِّعَتْ بِهِ ٱلْأَرْضُ أَوْ كُلِّمَ بِهِ ٱلْمَوْتَىٰ بَل لِّلَّهِ ٱلْأَمْرُ جَمِيعًا

"**If by a Qurʾān the mountains were made to move, or the earth were torn open, or the dead were made to speak** (they still would not believe). **No — the whole affair belongs to God.**"

**The verse answers the demand for a miracle, and the form of the answer must be recorded:** three extraordinary events are enumerated **and the sentence is cut off before it is completed** (*bal li'llāhi'l-amru jamīʿā*).

**The lexicographers record this cutting off (*ḥadhf*) and supply the rest of the unfinished sentence from the context: "they still would not believe."**

**I record this as an observation about the word order:** that the consequence is left unsaid is itself the answer. **And the same demand was treated at `025-furkan.md` 25/21 (*law lā unzila ʿalayna'l-malāʾika*);** `018-kehf.md` 18/57 was on the same line. **I rely on that treatment.**

---

## 13/32-38 — وَلَقَدِ ٱسْتُهْزِئَ بِرُسُلٍ مِّن قَبْلِكَ … أَفَمَنْ هُوَ قَآئِمٌ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ نَفْسٍۭ بِمَا كَسَبَتْ … مَّثَلُ ٱلْجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِى وُعِدَ ٱلْمُتَّقُونَ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُ أُكُلُهَا دَآئِمٌ وَظِلُّهَا

"Messengers before you were mocked as well… **He who stands over every soul with what it has earned** (is he like another?)… **This is the likeness of the Garden promised to those who guard themselves: rivers flow beneath it, and its produce and its shade are unceasing.**"

**أُكُلُهَا دَآئِمٌ وَظِلُّهَا** — **two things unceasing: the produce and the shade.**

**I record this as my own reading, and its ground is the sūra's own verses:** in the fourth verse the difference in **produce** (*ukul*) was named as a testing; **in the fifteenth verse the shadow** was counted among those that prostrate. **The thirty-fifth verse gives both of them as lasting.** It is a weave that can be verified within the sūra.

**وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيْنَٰهُمُ ٱلْكِتَٰبَ يَفْرَحُونَ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيْكَ وَمِنَ ٱلْأَحْزَابِ مَن يُنكِرُ بَعْضَهُۥ** (36)

**A note, per the method (`STYLE.md`):** the verse **issues no blanket ruling** about those who were given the Book — it says that some of them rejoice, and that among the parties there are some who deny part of it. **The same care was treated at `029-ankebut.md` 29/47;** I rely on that treatment.

**وَلَقَدْ أَرْسَلْنَا رُسُلًا مِّن قَبْلِكَ وَجَعَلْنَا لَهُمْ أَزْوَٰجًا وَذُرِّيَّةً** (38) — "**We sent messengers before you as well, and gave them wives and offspring.**"

**This answer was treated at `025-furkan.md` 25/20 (*yaʾkulūna'ṭ-ṭaʿāma wa yamshūna fi'l-aswāq*) and at `021-enbiya.md` 21/7-8.** **Three sūras answer the same objection with the same structure: that the messengers were human beings is set down not as a defect but as the rule.**

**وَمَا كَانَ لِرَسُولٍ أَن يَأْتِىَ بِـَٔايَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِ ٱللَّهِ لِكُلِّ أَجَلٍ كِتَابٌ** — **and the answer to the demand for a miracle: the authority does not lie with the messenger.** **The same demand had occurred twice at `013-rad.md` 13/7 and 13/27; here it comes a third time and closes with a ruling.**

## 13/39 — يَمْحُوا۟ ٱللَّهُ مَا يَشَآءُ وَيُثْبِتُ وَعِندَهُۥٓ أُمُّ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ

"**God erases what He wills and confirms what He wills. With Him is the mother of the Book.**"

The root **م-ح-و**: **to erase, to wipe out the trace of.** The root **ث-ب-ت**: **to fix in place and make firm.**

**أُمُّ ٱلْكِتَٰب** — **the construction was treated at `043-zuhruf.md` 43/4** (the root أ-م-م: origin, source, the place returned to). **I rely on that treatment.**

**There is disagreement in the classical commentaries over the scope of the verse:**

| Reading | What is erased and confirmed |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Rulings** — in the context of abrogation |
| 2 | **The records of deeds** |
| 3 | **General** — the working of the decree |

**All three readings are transmitted; I do not impose a preference, and I do not enter the debate on the divine decree.** **What the verse says in its own wording is that the erasing and the confirming are in one and the same hand.**

---

## 13/40 — وَإِن مَّا نُرِيَنَّكَ بَعْضَ ٱلَّذِى نَعِدُهُمْ أَوْ نَتَوَفَّيَنَّكَ فَإِنَّمَا عَلَيْكَ ٱلْبَلَٰغُ وَعَلَيْنَا ٱلْحِسَابُ

"**Whether We show you part of what We promise them or take you in death: yours is only the delivering of the message; the reckoning is Ours.**"

### The clearest statement of the limit

**The sentence draws a firm division between two tasks:**

| To whom | The task |
|---|---|
| *ʿAlayka* | **Al-Balāgh** — the delivering |
| *ʿAlaynā* | **Al-Ḥisāb** — the reckoning |

**And both clauses are built on the same pattern** (*innamā ʿalayka … wa ʿalaynā*) — **the division, that is, is shown by the word order as well.**

**The word *balāgh* was treated in detail at `046-ahkaf.md` 46/35**, and it was recorded there: **the sūra limits its own task to a single word.** **I rely on that treatment.**

**And this limit has occurred in many sūras in the index under different words; a table covering seven sūras was set up at `028-kasas.md` 28/56** (*balāgh*, *wakīl*, *jabbār*, *ḥasarāt*, *lā yaḥzunka*, *lā tahdī man aḥbabta*, *musayṭir*). **What al-Raʿd adds is this, and it is worth recording: here it is not only the limit that is set down — what lies on the other side of the limit is stated too: *wa ʿalayna'l-ḥisāb*.**

**I record this as my own reading:** in the others it was said "this is not your task"; **here "whose task it is" is stated as well.** The place to which the responsibility passes is not left empty.

## 13/41-43 — أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا۟ أَنَّا نَأْتِى ٱلْأَرْضَ نَنقُصُهَا مِنْ أَطْرَافِهَا … وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ لَسْتَ مُرْسَلًا قُلْ كَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ شَهِيدًۢا بَيْنِى وَبَيْنَكُمْ وَمَنْ عِندَهُۥ عِلْمُ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ

"**Have they not seen that We come to the earth and diminish it at its edges?**… Those who disbelieve say: '**You are not one who has been sent.**' Say: '**God is sufficient as a witness between me and you, and whoever has the knowledge of the Book.**'"

**نَنقُصُهَا مِنْ أَطْرَافِهَا** — "**we diminish it at its edges.**"

**There is disagreement in the classical commentaries over what the expression describes:**

| Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Lands changing hands** — the narrowing of the area of dominion |
| 2 | **Deaths** — the diminishing of people |
| 3 | **A general statement of diminution** |

**All three readings are transmitted; I do not impose a preference.** **Per the method (`STYLE.md`): I draw no conclusion about geology or about current politics from this.**

**وَمَنْ عِندَهُۥ عِلْمُ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ** — **the sūra closes with the phrase "whoever has the knowledge of the Book".**

**I record this as an observation, and its ground is the first verse of the sūra:** the sūra had opened with *tilka āyātu'l-kitāb* (these are the verses of the Book). **At the close the Book is named again — this time as a witness.**
